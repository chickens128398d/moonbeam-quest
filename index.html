A unicorn’s field guide

Move with WASD / arrow keys or the touch joystick. Hold Space / SHOOT to fire at nearby storms. Use Pause or P / Escape to take a break.

Explore a new, scrolling maze in each room. The corner map reveals places you have explored. Pink dots are storms; gold marks your unlocked exit. Clear the storms, then follow the rainbow door on the right. Each level adds one more storm, starting with six. Room 7 is one open box with the thunder boss.

Every new room restores 100 health. Storms deal 3 damage; the boss deals 10. Moonbeam unlocks in room 3 and Sunshine in room 5. Beat the boss and collect the pie to enter the party room! Tap Space or the mobile JUMP / FLY button to jump. Hold to fly and poop glitter. Let go to land and eat endless pie. Pause to start a new adventure.
